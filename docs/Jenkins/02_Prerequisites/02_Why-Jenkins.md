# Why Jenkins

Jenkins remains a popular CI/CD tool due to its versatility, cost-effectiveness, and broad ecosystem of plugins. In this article, we'll explore the key benefits that make Jenkins a standout choice among CI/CD solutions.

## Open Source and Cost-Effective

Jenkins is an open source platform that is completely free to use. You can deploy it on a virtual machine, run it inside a container, or launch it locally for development. Although Jenkins can be installed on platforms like macOS or Windows, these environments are generally not recommended for production use. One of Jenkins' greatest advantages is that it delivers a comprehensive CI/CD suite, complete with an extensive range of plugins, without any licensing fees.

> [!important]
> **Key Point**
>
> Jenkins offers a full-featured CI/CD solution with zero licensing costs, making it ideal for developers and organizations looking to streamline their development pipelines inexpensively.

## Extensive Plugin Ecosystem

A major strength of Jenkins is its rich plugin ecosystem, with over a thousand available plugins. This extensive library gives Jenkins a significant edge over many competing platforms. Its long-standing presence in the open source community has resulted in contributions from developers worldwide. This means that you can find compatible plugins for nearly every major DevOps tool, public cloud provider, and programming language—whether you’re using Go, Python, or others. Moreover, if you need a specific functionality that isn’t already available, Jenkins empowers you to develop your own plugin.

## Enterprise Options

For organizations that need enterprise-level support, Jenkins also offers robust options. Enterprise solutions for Jenkins provide professional support, managed services, and a SaaS-like experience—all designed to remove the complexities of self-hosting. These options are perfect for businesses that require a more managed and secure environment while still benefiting from the flexibility of Jenkins.

![The image showcases CloudBees' enterprise options, highlighting support and managed services, with a focus on continuous integration for enterprises using Jenkins.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752880116/notes-assets/images/Jenkins-Why-Jenkins/frame_120.jpg)

That concludes our overview of why Jenkins stands out in the CI/CD landscape. We look forward to sharing more insights and technical guidance in future posts.
