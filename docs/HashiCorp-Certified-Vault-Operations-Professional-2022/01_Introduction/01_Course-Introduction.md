## Exam Overview

This is a four-hour, online-proctored exam combining hands-on Vault configuration tasks with multiple-choice questions. You’ll be tested on deploying, configuring, managing, and monitoring Vault in real-world scenarios. Ideal candidates hold the Vault Associate certification or have equivalent production experience.
