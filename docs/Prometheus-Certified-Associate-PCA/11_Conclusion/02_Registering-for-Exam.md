# Registering for Exam

To register for the PCA exam, follow the steps below for a smooth enrollment process via the Linux Foundation Training site.

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> In this demonstration, the process uses a Google account for login, but you can use any preferred method to sign in.

1.  Visit the Linux Foundation training site at [training.linuxfoundation.org](https://training.linuxfoundation.org) and click on **My Training Portal** to log in.

    ![The image shows a login page for The Linux Foundation's Training & Certification, with options to sign in using an email or social media accounts. There is also a link to create a new account.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752882970/notes-assets/images/Prometheus-Certified-Associate-PCA-Registering-for-Exam/linux-foundation-login-page.jpg)

2.  Once logged in, you will arrive at the main page. Use the search bar to look for the "PCA" exam. A single result should appear, displaying details such as the exam cost. Click on the result for more information.
3.  On the exam details page, click the **Enroll Now** button to start the enrollment process. You will be redirected to the checkout page where you need to enter your payment details.

    ![The image shows a webpage for the Prometheus Certified Associate (PCA) course offered by the Linux Foundation, with details about the course and an "Enroll Now" button.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752882974/notes-assets/images/Prometheus-Certified-Associate-PCA-Registering-for-Exam/prometheus-certified-associate-course.jpg)

4.  After your payment has been processed, you will be taken to the registration completion page. Here, provide any additional required information such as your identification details and select your preferred exam date. Once you finish these steps, your registration will be confirmed and you will be scheduled for your exam on the chosen date.

For further information on the exam policies and preparation resources, please refer to the official Linux Foundation documentation.
