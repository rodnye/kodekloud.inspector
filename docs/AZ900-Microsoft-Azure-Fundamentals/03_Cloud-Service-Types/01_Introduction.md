# Introduction

Bella Innovation has developed a crucial invoicing application that streamlines billing processes by ensuring efficient and accurate client invoicing. As the business grows, the company is now exploring cloud computing to enhance reliability, scalability, and overall management of this essential solution.

Considering cloud service models, the focus is on three primary options:

1.  **Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)**
2.  **Platform as a Service (PaaS)**
3.  **Software as a Service (SaaS)**

![The image is a diagram titled "How to Move Forward," showing three types of services: Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Software as a Service (SaaS).](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752868244/notes-assets/images/AZ900-Microsoft-Azure-Fundamentals-Introduction/how-to-move-forward-iaas-paas-saas.jpg)

## Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

The following sections will examine Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) in detail. This option allows companies to leverage virtualized computing resources over the internet, providing flexibility and control over the underlying hardware and networking components. It offers a scalable and cost-effective alternative to managing physical servers on-premises.

> [!important]
> **Key Benefit**
>
> IaaS empowers you with enhanced scalability and operational flexibility, making it an excellent choice for businesses experiencing rapid growth or requiring dynamic workload management.
