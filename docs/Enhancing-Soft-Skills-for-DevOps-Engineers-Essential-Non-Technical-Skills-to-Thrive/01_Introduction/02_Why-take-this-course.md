Introduction

# Why take this course

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> Enhancing non-technical skills is just as critical as mastering tools in modern DevOps teams.

Welcome! I’m Michael Forrester. In this lesson, we’ll explore why strong soft skills—especially communication—are indispensable for DevOps engineers, and how this course will help you excel.

## 1\. Effective Communication Drives Team Productivity

Research from [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com), [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com), and [Think Talent](https://thinktalent.com) shows that improving team communication can increase productivity by 20–25%. Organizations with top-tier communication practices achieve up to 3.5× higher output, better alignment, and superior quality—directly satisfying customer expectations.

## 2\. Collaboration Boosts Workplace Satisfaction

When team members build genuine connections beyond task-related discussions, trust and engagement soar. A collaborative environment feels like a community, driving satisfaction and retention.

![The image illustrates the concept that communication increases workplace satisfaction, highlighting efficient collaboration and community building. It includes colorful icons representing people and a person speaking in the bottom right corner.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752874589/notes-assets/images/Enhancing-Soft-Skills-for-DevOps-Engineers-Essential-Non-Technical-Skills-to-Thrive-Why-take-this-course/communication-workplace-satisfaction-collaboration.jpg)

## 3\. Clear Communication Forges Trust

Trust emerges when commitments are honored and values are shared. Consistency in messaging ensures every team member aligns with organizational goals, creating a foundation for high performance.

![The image is a slide titled "Communication Creates Trust," featuring a shield with a checkmark and colorful icons representing people. ## 5\. People Are Harder to Manage Than Technology

With nearly 30 years in IT—from help desk roles to positions at ThoughtWorks, Red Hat, and AWS—I’ve found that team dynamics often present bigger challenges than coding or Kubernetes operations. Mastering soft skills accelerates career growth far beyond technical expertise alone.
