# Objective 5 Section Overview

In this lesson, you’ll learn how to protect and recover your Consul cluster data by covering:

- How to inspect the contents of a Consul snapshot
- Using snapshots to back up and restore an entire datacenter
- An introduction to the Consul Snapshot Agent (Enterprise feature)

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> Regular snapshots are vital for disaster recovery and maintaining high availability in your Consul deployment.
