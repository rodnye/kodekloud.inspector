# Section Introduction

Welcome to Module 10: Design a Business Continuity Solution. In this lesson, we delve into creating a robust business continuity framework by focusing on backup and recovery strategies across a range of Azure services.

We will cover the following Azure services:

- Azure Blob Storage
- Azure Files
- Azure Virtual Machines
- SQL Databases

In addition, this module introduces the disaster recovery solution, Azure Site Recovery, an essential tool for ensuring minimal downtime and data integrity in the event of service disruption.

> [!important]
> **Course Reference**
>
> Some of these topics have been covered in detail in our [AZ-104: Microsoft Azure Administrator](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/az-104-microsoft-azure-administrator) course. This lesson serves as a concise review while introducing additional concepts to further enhance your understanding of designing comprehensive backup and recovery solutions.

Let’s embark on the journey to explore effective backup and recovery designs that will safeguard your data and empower your organization to maintain business continuity.
