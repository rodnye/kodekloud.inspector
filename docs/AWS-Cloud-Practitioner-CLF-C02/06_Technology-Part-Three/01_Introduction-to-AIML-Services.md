Before exploring AI and ML in detail, it is essential to understand the role of AWS (Amazon Web Services). AWS is renowned as an on-demand IT delivery platform that provides API-driven services. For this discussion, envision AWS as a vast, versatile toolbox. ## What Are AI and ML?

To move forward, it’s important to clarify the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning:

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** The capability of computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A subset of AI that uses algorithms and statistical models to enable machines to learn from data and make predictions or decisions without explicit instructions.

In simple terms, think of AI as the overall ability of a machine to perform human-like tasks, while ML is the methodology that trains the machine to acquire new skills.

![The image defines AI as Artificial Intelligence and ML as Machine Learning, with a title asking "What are AI and ML?"](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752862108/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Introduction-to-AIML-Services/frame_110.jpg)

Visualize it like this: AI is the finished dish—say, a flavorful spaghetti—while ML is the secret recipe that brings the dish to life. AWS’s ML services act as the magic wands executing these recipes, handling tasks such as chatbot deployment, data analysis, and transcription with ease.

![The image explains AI (Artificial Intelligence) and ML (Machine Learning) with icons, highlighting their relationship and differences.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752862109/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Introduction-to-AIML-Services/frame_140.jpg)

Imagine yourself as a chef preparing a delightful meal. In this analogy, AI represents the essential ingredients—the garlic, basil, and oregano—that add flavor, whereas ML is your trusted recipe, orchestrating these ingredients into a cohesive and satisfying experience.
