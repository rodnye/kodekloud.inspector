## What You'll Learn

This course is structured into seven comprehensive sections that cover the fundamentals of AWS. Here’s a quick overview of the key topics:

- **Core AWS Concepts:** Dive into digital billing, pricing fundamentals, and cost analysis tools.
- **Cloud Design Principles:** Learn scalable and resilient architecture strategies.
- **AWS Services:** Gain hands-on insight into services like RDS, DynamoDB, Systems Manager, CloudTrail, EC2, S3, and VPC.
- **Security Essentials:** Understand Identity and Access Management (IAM) best practices and the AWS shared responsibility model.
- **Knowledge Assessments:** Enhance your understanding through various quizzes and pre-/post-assessments tailored to prepare you for the AWS exam.
