Introduction

# Why take this Course AWS CP

Welcome to this lesson! I'm Michael Forrester, and I'll guide you through the overall structure and layout of the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification course, recently updated to version CLFC02. Let's dive into the details and explore how this course can help you achieve your certification goals.

## Course Objectives

This course is meticulously crafted for the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification exam. It covers the essential topics needed to excel in your exam—the foundational certification in the AWS ecosystem. The four key focus areas are:

![The image outlines course objectives, including Cloud Concepts, Security, Billing and Pricing, and Tech and Services, connected by a key symbol.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861675/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_40.jpg)

1.  **Basic Cloud Concepts:** Understand cloud design principles, economic benefits, and overall advantages of cloud computing.
2.  **Security:** Delve into critical security aspects and best practices to safeguard your AWS environment.
3.  **Billing and Pricing:** Although this is a smaller focus—approximately an eighth of the exam—it is crucial to understand AWS billing mechanisms.
4.  **Technology and Services:** This is the most expansive section, addressing a broad range of AWS services and technologies.

## Who Is This Course Designed For?

This course is ideal for beginners. If you have a basic understanding of computing and networking, you’re ready to embark on this learning journey. Prior experience with any cloud provider (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud) is not required. While some basic knowledge of operating systems and laptop components (like memory, hard drives, or processors) can be helpful, it isn’t essential.

![The image shows a Venn diagram with "Operating System" and "Laptop Components," highlighting their intersection, under the title "Who Is It Designed For?"](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861676/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_120.jpg)

## Course Structure

The course is organized into eight comprehensive sections along with two assessments to ensure your understanding and progress:

1.  **Introduction:**  
    Here, you'll learn about the course objectives, structure, and context. A pre-assessment is provided to establish your starting point.

    > [!important]
    > **Note**
    >
    > It is highly recommended to take the pre-assessment. Even if you perform well, it may highlight areas where further focus is needed.

2.  **Cloud Computing:**  
    Explore cloud economics, design principles, and a variety of deployment models (private, public, or hybrid). This section also explains the six key benefits of cloud computing, including global scalability and enhanced business agility.

    ![The image shows a course outline for cloud computing, including sections on benefits, deployment models, cloud economics, and design principles.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861677/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_200.jpg)

3.  **Security and Compliance:**  
    Gain a solid understanding of AWS security fundamentals. This section covers the shared responsibility model and effective identity access management techniques, ensuring you know how to secure your AWS resources.
4.  **Technology and Services:**  
    This extensive section is divided into three parts:
    - **Part One:** Focuses on AWS core services, emphasizing infrastructure, such as networking, storage, and compute resources.
    - **Part Two:** Covers additional core technologies, including databases and management tools.
    - **Part Three:** Introduces emerging topics like machine learning, business applications, and developer tools—critical additions for the CLFC02 exam.

    ![The image outlines an AWS course with 8 sections and 2 assessments, focusing on technology, infrastructure, and fundamental services.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861678/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_240.jpg)

    ![The image shows a course outline for AWS, focusing on machine learning and business applications, with eight sections and two assessments.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861680/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_270.jpg)

5.  **Billing and Pricing:**  
    Understand how billing is managed within AWS—from locating your bill to the payment process, ensuring transparency in cost management.

    ![The image shows a course outline for AWS, featuring "Billing and Pricing" as a section, with a total of 8 sections and 2 assessments.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861681/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_300.jpg)

6.  **Conclusion and Next Steps:**  
    In this final section, you will review the course material, complete the post-assessment, and be guided towards additional resources to continue your AWS learning journey.

The course incorporates both pre- and post-assessments.

> [!important]
> **Assessment Guidance**
>
> The pre-assessment is optional but recommended for self-evaluation, while the post-assessment should ideally reach a score of over 90% after the course. Both assessments mirror the actual exam format—with 65 questions to be answered within 90 minutes.

![The image outlines a course's pre- and post-assessments, featuring 65 questions to be completed in 90 minutes, with "Before" and "After" sections highlighted.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861682/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_360.jpg)

Across the six major sections (Cloud Concepts, Security, the three parts of Technology and Services, and Billing and Pricing), you will encounter a 25-question quiz after each section. These quizzes are designed to reinforce the material covered and ensure you have a firm grasp on the concepts.

![The image outlines a course section with quizzes, featuring 25 questions across 6 sections, and includes a checklist icon.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752861683/notes-assets/images/AWS-Cloud-Practitioner-CLF-C02-Why-take-this-Course-AWS-CP/frame_390.jpg)

## Study Recommendations

Pacing your study sessions is key to success. Here are some tips to help you stay on track:

- **Set a Consistent Schedule:** Develop a routine, whether it's studying 15–30 minutes daily or longer sessions on weekends. Consistency will help reinforce your learning.
- **Engage Actively:** Instead of passively reading, interact with demos, supplementary articles, and discussion forums.
- **Plan Ahead:** Reserve dedicated, undisturbed study time in your calendar to maximize concentration and comprehension.

## Summary

The AWS Cloud Practitioner course is structured into eight key sections:

- An introductory section with a pre-assessment.
- Six core sections covering cloud concepts, security, the three-part technology and services module, and billing/pricing—with each section featuring a quiz.
- A concluding section with a post-assessment to measure your progress and readiness.

This course is perfect for beginners, offering a comprehensive path to not only prepare for the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification exam but also to develop a disciplined study routine.
