## Why Multiple Storage Options?

Modern applications generate diverse data types—media, transactional records, logs, and analytics. Azure’s comprehensive storage portfolio lets you:

- Scale virtually without limits
- Ensure high availability with multi-region replication
- Optimize costs through tiered pricing and lifecycle management
