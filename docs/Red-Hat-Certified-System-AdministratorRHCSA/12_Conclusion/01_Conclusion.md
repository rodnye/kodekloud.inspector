# Conclusion

Congratulations on completing your Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) Preparation Course!

Now that you've finished the course, you're well-prepared to take the RHCSA exam and earn your certification. Before you register for the exam, remember that you need to create an account with Red Hat and purchase your exam voucher. For more details on getting started, please visit the [Red Hat Certified System Administrator Exam](https://redhat.com/en/services/training/ex200-red-hat-certified-system-administrator-rhcsa-exam) page and click "Get Started".

![The image shows a webpage for the Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) exam, detailing the exam format, duration, cost, and offering updates about remote testing options.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752883537/notes-assets/images/Red-Hat-Certified-System-AdministratorRHCSA-Conclusion/rhcsa-exam-format-details-webpage.jpg)

If you plan to take the exam remotely, click "Find Out More" on the webpage and download the free ebook available at [Red Hat Remote Exams Preparation](https://redhat.com/en/remote-exams/preparation/ebook). It is highly recommended that you download the live exam environment and test it on your machine several days before your scheduled exam. This proactive step will ensure that your hardware is compatible with the live exam environment and will help you troubleshoot any potential issues in advance.

![The image shows a webpage from Red Hat offering an e-book titled "Get ready for your Red Hat remote exam," with a download button and navigation links.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752883538/notes-assets/images/Red-Hat-Certified-System-AdministratorRHCSA-Conclusion/red-hat-remote-exam-ebook.jpg)

> [!important]
> **Exam Preparation Tip**
>
> Before exam day, make sure to test your live exam environment. This will help prevent any last-minute technical difficulties and ensure a smooth exam experience.

Once you pass the RHCSA exam, Red Hat will send you an email notification. You can then log in to your training portal account to view your score and exam results.

Thank you for following along with this lesson. I hope you enjoyed the course as much as I enjoyed teaching it, and I wish you the best of luck on your exam!
