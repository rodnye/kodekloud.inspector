# Course Introduction

Hello and welcome to the DevOps Interview Preparation Course!

My name is Raghunandana Sanur, and I'll be your guide throughout this comprehensive series designed to boost your confidence and preparedness for DevOps interviews.

In this course, we explore a wide range of DevOps technologies, focusing on effectively answering commonly asked interview questions. Whether you are preparing for your first DevOps role or looking to update your existing skills, this course is tailored for you. It is recommended that participants have a basic understanding of DevOps concepts or prior IT experience before diving in.

![A person is speaking with a caption that reads, "You need to be aware of DevOps technologies."](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752873366/notes-assets/images/DevOps-Interview-Preparation-Course-Course-Introduction/devops-technologies-awareness-speech.jpg)

The course is structured to maximize learning by following these steps:

1.  **Presentation of a question:** Each segment begins with a carefully selected question.
2.  **Explaining the motivation:** I will share why an interviewer might ask this particular question.
3.  **Defining key terminologies:** Important terms will be briefly defined to ensure clarity.
4.  **Structured answer approach:** I will then outline a methodical way to answer the question, mirroring the process used during actual interviews.

Thank you.
