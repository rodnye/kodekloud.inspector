# Cost Models

At Cursor AI, we offer straightforward, tiered pricing designed to scale with your AI coding assistant needs—from individual hobbyists to enterprise teams. Start with the essentials and upgrade as your projects grow or you unlock the productivity benefits of our advanced features.

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> Pricing tiers are subject to change. Visit [Cursor AI Pricing](https://cursor.ai/pricing) for the latest details.

![The image displays three pricing plan options: Tiered Structure, User-Focused Design, and Scalable Solutions, each with brief descriptions of their features.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752872701/notes-assets/images/Cursor-AI-Cost-Models/pricing-plans-tiered-user-focused-scalable.jpg)

## Hobby Plan: Code for Free

Perfect for students, hobbyists, or anyone who wants to explore AI-assisted coding without commitment.

- 2,000 code completions
- 50 premium requests (slow-mode access)
- 200 uses of the Cursor Small model
- Includes a two-week Pro trial

Models run at reduced speed compared to paid tiers, but still offer invaluable support for personal projects and learning exercises.

![The image describes a "Hobby Plan: Code for Free" offering three options: a 2-week pro trial, 2,000 code completions, and 50 premium requests, each with specific features for users.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752872702/notes-assets/images/Cursor-AI-Cost-Models/hobby-plan-code-for-free-options.jpg)

## Pro Plan: Unlimited Productivity

Cursor AI’s most popular plan, designed for individual professional developers who need fast, reliable assistance.

- Unlimited code completions
- 500 fast premium requests
- 10 AI mini daily requests
- Unlimited slow premium requests
- Priced at $20 /user per month

Fast requests keep you moving on deadlines, mini daily grants access to cutting-edge models, and unlimited slow requests mean you’re never blocked.

![The image is an advertisement for a "Pro Plan" subscription costing $20/month, offering features like unlimited completions, 500 fast premium requests, 10 o1-mini daily, and unlimited slow premium requests.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752872704/notes-assets/images/Cursor-AI-Cost-Models/pro-plan-subscription-advertisement.jpg)

## Business Plan: Enterprise Power

Building on Pro features with robust team and security capabilities for organizations of all sizes.

- Everything in the Pro Plan
- Organization-wide privacy mode
- Centralized team billing
- Admin dashboard with usage statistics
- SAML, OIDC, and SSO integration
- Priced at $40 /user per month

Ideal for development teams that require advanced governance, single sign-on, and detailed analytics.

![The image outlines features of a business plan called "Enterprise Power," including privacy controls, centralized billing, an admin dashboard, SSO integration, and additional pro features.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752872706/notes-assets/images/Cursor-AI-Cost-Models/enterprise-power-business-plan-features.jpg)

## Annual Savings

Lock in a 20% discount when you commit for a year—two months free compared to monthly billing.

| Plan     | Monthly Cost   | Annual Cost (20% Off) | Savings         |
| -------- | -------------- | --------------------- | --------------- |
| Pro      | $20 /user / mo | $192 /user / yr       | Two months free |
| Business | $40 /user / mo | $384 /user / yr       | Two months free |

![The image promotes savings with an annual commitment, offering a 20% discount and a yearly pro cost of $192, which includes two free months.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752872708/notes-assets/images/Cursor-AI-Cost-Models/annual-savings-20-percent-discount.jpg)

## Flexible Negotiation Options

We tailor subscriptions to your needs—no rigid pricing structures.

- Auto-renewal control (manual or automatic)
- Overage-fee discussions for fluctuating usage
- Discount continuity for existing customers

> [!important]
> **Tip**
>
> Discuss auto-renewal, overage fees, or enterprise discounts with our sales team to tailor your subscription.

## Finding Your Perfect Plan

1.  Assess your coding volume and team size.
2.  Evaluate premium model usage to decide between fast vs. slow requests.
3.  Compare monthly flexibility vs. annual savings.
4.  Reach out for custom enterprise bundles tailored to your requirements.
