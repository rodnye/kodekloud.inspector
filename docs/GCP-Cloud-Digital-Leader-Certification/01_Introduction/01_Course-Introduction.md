# Course Introduction

Hello and welcome to the GCP Digital Leader Certification course! I’m Raghunandana Sanur, and I will be your instructor on this journey to mastering Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

Google Cloud Platform has quickly become a major player in the cloud services market, offering an extensive suite of powerful tools and services. Its rapid evolution allows organizations to undergo complete digital transformations by leveraging advanced GCP services, including cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) capabilities.

There are no prerequisites to begin this course. The material provided is designed to thoroughly prepare you for the GCP Digital Leader Certification exam. Key focus areas include:

- GCP's role in digitally transforming organizations.
- A diverse range of GCP services, such as compute resources, databases, and more.
- Innovative tools available for implementing AI and Machine Learning solutions.

> [!important]
> **Course Environment**
>
> During the lab sessions, we recommend using your personal GCP account or the KodeKloud Playground for hands-on practice. If you encounter any issues or have questions, please reach out via the KodeKloud community or our dedicated Slack channel.

We continually update this course to incorporate the latest GCP services and features as they are released. To stay current, please revisit this article periodically for updates and new learning resources.

I am excited to guide you on this journey. Let’s dive into the course content and start your path toward becoming a certified GCP Digital Leader.

Thank you for joining us!
