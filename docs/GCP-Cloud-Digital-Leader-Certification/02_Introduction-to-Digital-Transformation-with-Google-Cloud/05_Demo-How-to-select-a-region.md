# Demo How to select a region

Previously, we discussed the global infrastructure of GCP. In this article, we explore a handy GCP tool called the Region Picker. This tool helps you choose the optimal region for your application by evaluating three key parameters:

- Lower carbon footprint
- Lower price
- Lower latency

Let's dive into a demonstration of how to use this tool.

## How to Use the Region Picker

1.  Open your browser and search for "GCP Region Picker". Click on the first link from the search results.
2.  On the landing page, you will notice two panels:
    - **Left Panel:** Contains adjustable parameters.
    - **Right Panel:** Displays a list of recommended regions.

> [!important]
> **Tip**
>
> Try experimenting with different settings. For example, increase the importance of having a lower carbon footprint, emphasize cost efficiency, or prioritize lower latency.

## Understanding Parameter Adjustments

When you adjust the parameters, consider the origin of your traffic. For instance:

- If you select a region far away from your user base—say, the United Kingdom—you might see recommendations like Finland (Europe North One) as the optimal region. The tool may also suggest other regions, such as Belgium and locations in the USA.

![The image shows a Google Cloud Region Picker tool, which helps users select a cloud region based on carbon footprint, price, and latency. It displays recommended regions, including locations in Finland, Belgium, the USA, and Canada.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752875341/notes-assets/images/GCP-Cloud-Digital-Leader-Certification-Demo-How-to-select-a-region/google-cloud-region-picker-tool.jpg)

Alternatively, if you choose a different starting region such as Switzerland, the tool might then recommend Belgium as the best option. This demonstrates that the optimal region is closely tied to the origin of your incoming traffic.

## Why Use the Region Picker?

This tool is invaluable if you want to ensure you're selecting the best region to accommodate your application's needs or if you're uncertain about where to deploy. By adjusting key parameters, you can balance factors like environmental impact, cost, and latency effectively.
