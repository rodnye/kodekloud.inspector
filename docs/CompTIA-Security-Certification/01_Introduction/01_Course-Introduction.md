# Course Introduction

Welcome to the CompTIA Security+ course—your gateway to mastering essential cybersecurity skills and safeguarding IT environments. If you're ready to enhance your cybersecurity capabilities and defend against today's evolving threats, then you've come to the right place.

This course blends theoretical concepts with interactive browser-based labs, allowing you to immediately apply new knowledge.

Overview of the course modules:

## Security Controls

Explore various types of security controls and how they form the foundation of a robust security framework.

## Core Security Concepts

Delve into essential concepts such as the CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability), nonrepudiation, AAA (Authentication, Authorization, Accounting), and the Zero Trust model.

## Physical Security and Specialized Topics

Learn strategies for securing the physical environment and supporting processes like change management and gap analysis.

## Cryptography

Understand the fundamentals of encryption, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), digital certificates, hashing, and salting techniques.

## Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations

Identify common threats and vulnerabilities, explore social engineering methods, and learn strategies for mitigation and hardening.

## Security Operations

Examine key operational tasks—from vulnerability management to automation—and how they support ongoing security posture maintenance.

## Security Architecture and Management

Learn to design and manage secure infrastructures, governance policies, risk management frameworks, and emerging technologies in security.

## Community and Enrollment

Join our KodeKloud community to collaborate with peers, share insights, and accelerate your learning. Enroll now and take your first step toward becoming a CompTIA Certified Security Professional.
