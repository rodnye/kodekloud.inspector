# Course Introduction

Welcome to the Docker Certified Associate (DCA) exam preparation course by Mirantis. I’m **Mumshad Mannambeth**, and alongside **Yogesh Raheja**, we’ll guide you step by step—from the fundamentals to advanced Docker concepts—so you can confidently pass the DCA exam and apply best practices in your projects.

## Exam Objectives

Our curriculum is designed around the official exam blueprint. You’ll master:

- Installation & configuration
- Image management
- Storage & volumes
- Networking
- Security
- Orchestration

![The image lists objectives related to technology, including installation and configuration, image management, storage and volumes, networking, security, and orchestration, each represented by an icon.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752873972/notes-assets/images/Docker-Certified-Associate-Exam-Course-Course-Introduction/technology-objectives-icons-list.jpg)

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> All module objectives map directly to DCA exam domains. You’ll see a mix of visualizations, hands-on demos, and animations to reinforce each topic.

## Quizzes & Mock Exams

Throughout the course, interactive quizzes will reinforce what you learn. Near the end, you’ll take full-length mock exams that mirror the DCA’s multiple-choice format.

> [!important]
> **Warning**
>
> Mock exams are **timed** and structured like the real DCA. Treat them seriously to gauge your readiness.
