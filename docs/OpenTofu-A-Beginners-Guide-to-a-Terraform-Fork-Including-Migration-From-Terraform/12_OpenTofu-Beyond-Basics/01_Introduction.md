# Introduction

Welcome to the **OpenTofu Beyond the Basics** lesson. In this module, you'll dive deep into how OpenTofu is shaping the future of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and why it matters for modern DevOps practices.

In this lesson, you will explore:

- What is OpenTofu: core features, governance model, and community-driven development
- The rise of OpenTofu: key events and milestones in its evolution
- Transition strategies: best practices for migrating from Terraform to OpenTofu
- The future of IaC: upcoming enhancements and the roadmap for OpenTofu

> [!important]
> **Prerequisites**
>
> This lesson assumes you have a foundational understanding of infrastructure automation tools like [Terraform](https://www.terraform.io/). If you’re new to IaC, be sure to review the basics before proceeding.
