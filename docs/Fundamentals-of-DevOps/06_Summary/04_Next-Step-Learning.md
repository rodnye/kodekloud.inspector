# Next Step Learning

Congratulations on completing the course! You've thoroughly explored the materials, narratives, and technical insights that build a strong foundation in DevOps. Now, it's time to plan your continuous growth journey. Consider setting SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals for the next six months and one year that align with your career objectives.

> [!important]
> **Next Steps**
>
> To deepen your DevOps expertise, engage in continuous learning through reading, hands-on practice, and certification courses.

## Essential Reading Recommendations

For a deeper dive into DevOps culture, real-world case studies, and technical mastery, start with these four essential books:

1.  **The Phoenix Project** – The seminal work on DevOps.
2.  **The DevOps Handbook**
3.  **Accelerate**
4.  **The Unicorn Project**

![A person gestures while sitting in a room with plants, wearing a "KodeKloud" shirt. Two book covers, "The Phoenix Project" and "The DevOps Handbook," are displayed.](https://kodekloud.com/kk-media/image/upload/v1752874994/notes-assets/images/Fundamentals-of-DevOps-Next-Step-Learning/frame_50.jpg)

These books offer valuable insights into both the technical and cultural aspects of DevOps. Also, broaden your skills by reading _Team Topologies_ for better team collaboration strategies, and consider pursuing DevOps certifications. Enhancing communication skills through classics like _How to Win Friends and Influence People_ by Dale Carnegie can further boost your professional growth.

## Recommended Learning Path

If you feel you need to concentrate on certain areas, follow this learning path to build up your technical skills:

1.  Start with [Shell Scripts for Beginners](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/shell-scripts-for-beginners).
2.  Progress to [Jenkins](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/jenkins).
3.  Continue with the [Terraform Basics Training Course](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/terraform-basics-training-course).
4.  Advance with the [Ansible Advanced Course](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/ansible-advanced-course).
5.  Explore [Kubernetes for the Absolute Beginners - Hands-on Tutorial](https://learn.kodekloud.com/user/courses/kubernetes-for-the-absolute-beginners-hands-on-tutorial).
6.  Finally, dive into broader CI/CD concepts.
