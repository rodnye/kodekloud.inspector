# Values Importance

In this article, we delve into the core shared values that form the cultural foundation of DevOps. These guiding principles not only shape the internal dynamics of an organization but also define the structure and collaboration of teams across departments.

This comprehensive list is designed to serve as a model to help you pinpoint which values are most critical for your team and organization. Along with outlining each value, we also discuss specific practices that reinforce these principles, ensuring that they become integral to your everyday work culture.

> [!important]
> **Note**
>
> The values discussed here are not exhaustive; rather, they serve as a starting point for organizations looking to align their cultural and operational practices with the DevOps philosophy.
